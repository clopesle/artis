# Design System: ARTÍS

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "O Atelier de Precisão"**

The site should feel like entering a quiet design consultation where jewelry,
anatomy, and personal identity are considered with equal care. Its warmth comes
from tactile pale surfaces and human photography; its authority comes from
measured composition, fine construction marks, and concise language.

The system is premium but never ostentatious. It rejects both the dark,
hard-edged vocabulary of generic piercing studios and the predictable
beige-and-gold luxury template. Motion is choreographed sparingly around the
central before-and-after idea and becomes still when reduced motion is requested.

**Key Characteristics:**

- Warm daylight, tactile materials, and real skin texture
- Fine technical lines used as evidence of process, not decoration
- One dominant visual idea per viewport
- Asymmetric compositions with disciplined alignment
- Mobile-first pacing for the Instagram-to-WhatsApp journey

## Typography

**Display Font:** `[serif display to be chosen at implementation]`

**Body Font:** `[warm humanist sans to be chosen at implementation]`

**Character:** Display type should feel like a finely printed jewelry certificate,
while body copy should feel personal, contemporary, and effortless on a phone.

### Hierarchy

- **Display:** Light to regular weight, fluid and decisive, reserved for the
  central promise.
- **Headline:** Strong contrast from body text, compact enough to anchor asymmetric
  compositions.
- **Title:** Clear section and item titles without ornamental italics.
- **Body:** Comfortable mobile reading with a maximum line length of 68 characters.
- **Label:** Short navigation and action labels with restrained tracking. Never
  use uppercase for paragraph-length copy.

**The Quiet Contrast Rule.** Hierarchy comes from scale, proportion, and space,
never from a collection of decorative font styles.

## Elevation

The system is flat by default. Depth comes from tonal paper layers, image overlap,
fine complete borders, and occasional diffuse ambient shadow around photography.
Surfaces do not float merely to imitate application cards.

**The Surface Rule.** If a shadow makes a section look like a dashboard card,
remove it and restore hierarchy through composition.

## Components

Components will be documented after the first implementation pass. They must feel

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:

- **Do** make the personalized visualization the first idea visitors understand.
- **Do** use anatomically credible imagery with natural skin texture.
- **Do** reserve thin technical lines and circles for explaining the design
  process.
- **Do** create unmistakable primary actions with at least 44px touch targets and
  visible focus states.
- **Do** keep every customer-facing word in Brazilian Portuguese.

### Don't:

- **Don't** build a generic piercing-studio site with black backgrounds, neon
  accents, tattoo imagery, or aggressive visual language.
- **Don't** build template luxury from beige surfaces, generic serif headlines,
  gold gradients, and ornamental excess.
- **Don't** use generic AI imagery with implausible ears, invented jewelry,
  plastic skin, or anatomically incorrect piercing positions.
- **Don't** use a marketplace or conventional ecommerce layout that reduces
  ARTÍS to a product grid.
- **Don't** use dense institutional pages that explain everything before showing
  the central promise.
- **Don't** publish unsupported claims, fabricated testimonials, placeholder
  prices, or invented portfolio projects.
