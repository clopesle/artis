# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

ARTÍS serves sophisticated adult women, primarily arriving from Instagram on
mobile devices. They are considering new ear piercings or a more coherent
auricular composition and want to understand how the result could look before
making an aesthetic and anatomical commitment.

The visitor's job is to gain confidence, understand the personalized digital
design service, see evidence of care and taste, and begin a consultation through
WhatsApp.

## Product Purpose

The site presents ARTÍS as a Brazilian specialist in personalized digital
auricular design, visagism, and jewelry curation. It explains the online process,
shows the quality of the work, and converts qualified interest into contextual
WhatsApp conversations.

Success means a visitor quickly understands that ARTÍS plans a composition before
any piercing, trusts the brand's aesthetic and technical judgment, and contacts
ARTÍS about a personalized project. GitHub remains the sole persistent content
source, and the public experience remains compatible with static hosting.

## Brand Personality

Delicate, assured, and intentional.

ARTÍS speaks as a close, highly capable design consultant. The voice is warm and
feminine without becoming childish, luxurious without ostentation, and technical
without becoming clinical. It guides and inspires instead of applying sales
pressure.

## Anti-references

- Generic piercing-studio sites dominated by black backgrounds, neon accents,
  tattoo imagery, or aggressive visual language
- Template luxury expressed only through beige surfaces, generic serif headlines,
  gold gradients, and ornamental excess
- Generic AI imagery with implausible ears, invented jewelry, plastic skin, or
  anatomically incorrect piercing positions
- Marketplace and conventional ecommerce layouts that reduce ARTÍS to a product
  grid
- Dense institutional pages that explain everything before showing the central
  promise
- Unsupported claims, fabricated testimonials, placeholder prices, or invented
  portfolio projects

## Design Principles

1. **Show the decision before the transaction.** Lead with the confidence of
   visualizing a composition before piercing, then explain the service.
2. **Make expertise feel close.** Use plain Brazilian Portuguese, calm guidance,
   and context-rich WhatsApp actions rather than institutional or aggressive copy.
3. **Treat anatomy as a design constraint.** Never sacrifice anatomical
   credibility or the integrity of jewelry for decorative effect.
4. **Let one precise detail carry the page.** Favor decisive photography,
   disciplined typography, and purposeful space over repeated cards and
   ornamental UI.
5. **Keep every next step intentional.** Each page and major section leads toward
   understanding the process, viewing real work, or starting a consultation.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA where practical. Maintain readable contrast, semantic structure,
keyboard access, visible focus states, generous touch targets, reduced-motion
support, responsive typography, descriptive Brazilian Portuguese alternatives for
meaningful images, and no reliance on color alone. Photography and language should
represent a range of adult women and skin tones without objectification.
